HOW HIGH

How High is a 2001 American stoner comedy film directed by Jesse Dylan in his feature film directorial debut and written by Dustin Lee Abraham. In the film, Method Man and Redman portray two cannabis users who are visited by the ghost of a deceased friend after smoking his ashes; the ghost helps with their exams, and they receive scholarships to Harvard University.

How High was produced by Jersey Films and Native Pictures and distributed by Universal Pictures. The film was released on December 21, 2001, and received generally negative reviews from critics.[3][4] A sequel, titled How High 2, was released in 2019.
